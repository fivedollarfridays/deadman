## What landed

**\`src/deadman/verify/\`** — split in two deliberately:
- \`expectations.py:75\` — the declaration. It never sees a store, so a deadline has nowhere to come from except configuration. That's the module split doing the enforcement, not a comment asking nicely.
- \`collector_liveness.py:175\` — the verdicts.

**Two clocks, because there are two questions.** Collector liveness is about *delivery* (\`received_at\`); surface freshness is about *reading* (\`read_at\`). Store-and-forward makes that distinction real: a collector that lost the network for a day and then drained its spool is alive — it just shipped — while every reading in that spool is a day old. The board now says both.

**A stale surface reads \`UNOBSERVABLE\` whatever it last said, including FAULT.** Not a lost alarm — blindness is counted on its own line, the collector is faulting beside it, and the prior observation survives in \`detail\['last_observation'\]\`. What's refused is asserting a current verdict from a reading too old to be one.

**The board** gained \`collectors_declared\`, \`fresh_count\`, \`stale_count\`, \`unreported_count\`, \`undeclared_surfaces\` — additive, so the DM1 contract DM2.8 must stay compatible with is intact. The three surface counts partition what was declared, so "no faults" and "nothing reported" can't be the same number. \`sample-outputs/board.json\` regenerated by script.

**The mutation AC is a committed script, not a claim.** \`scripts/mutation_check.py\` inverts 11 guards one at a time and requires each to break one named test; a surviving mutation exits non-zero as an unprotected guard. All 11 caught. It restores sources in a \`finally\` plus an \`atexit\` hook — verified clean afterward.

## Two things worth your attention

**DM2.6 must set \`DEADMAN_COLLECTORS\` on the deploy.** Unset is legal and warns on stderr (mirroring \`default_store\`'s bargain for the forgetful backend), but a deployed service with ingest enabled and no declared collectors is exactly the false-green this task exists to kill. I chose warn-not-die because the service is also the DM1-era local board; if you'd rather it refuse to boot like the ingest secret does, that's a one-line change and I'd argue it's defensible.

**\`interval_seconds\` (900) is duplicated in the launchd plist's \`StartInterval\` (900).** Nothing enforces they match across the collector/service wire. DM2.6 owns cadence and will have to change both; I've recorded that in \`What's Next\` rather than build a reconciliation mechanism this task didn't ask for.
